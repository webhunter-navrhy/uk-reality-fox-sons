Location

Tollgate Road is conveniently positioned for access to local shops, amenities and regular bus routes, while being within easy reach of Salisbury city centre with its selection of restaurants, cafes, independent retailers and the historic Cathedral Close. The area benefits from good transport connections, making it a practical location for both commuters and those seeking a home close to the city.

Description

This detached two-bedroom bungalow is set within a popular residential area and requires modernisation, whilst benefiting from a single garage and driveway providing off-road parking for two vehicles.

Accommodation

Ground Floor: Entrance Porch, Entrance Hallway, Lounge, Kitchen, Bedroom One, Bedroom Two, Shower Room, Separate WC.

Outside: Gardens to front and side elevations. Driveway providing off road parking leading to single garage.
