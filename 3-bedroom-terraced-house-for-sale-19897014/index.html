DESCRIPTION

Tucked away behind an attractive brick frontage with a characterful arched entrance, this charming three-bedroom period home blends original features with practical family living. Offering over 1,000 sq ft of accommodation, the property enjoys off-road driveway for two cars, a useful outbuilding and a convenient position close to local amenities.

The accommodation begins with an entrance porch and hallway leading to a bright sitting room with period fireplace and front aspect window. A separate dining room provides an excellent space for entertaining and flows through to the kitchen/breakfast room, fitted with a range of shaker-style units and ample space for everyday dining. A downstairs toilet completes the ground floor. Upstairs, the landing serves three well-proportioned bedrooms, all benefiting from good natural light, with charming fireplaces retained in two rooms, alongside a family bathroom fitted with a bath and shower over. Outside, the property enjoys a landscaped frontage with off-road parking and an enclosed rear courtyard garden designed for low-maintenance enjoyment. A substantial timber outbuilding provides excellent storage, workshop space or potential for a hobby room or home gym.

Longland is well positioned for access to local shops, schools and amenities, while also offering convenient links to Salisbury city centre, the railway station and surrounding countryside, making it an excellent choice for families or professionals.
